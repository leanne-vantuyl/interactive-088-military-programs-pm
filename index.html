Sixteen Case Files.
One Facility.

An interactive, scenario-based companion to PROSPECT 088, built for Military Programs project managers. Follow Nora Delgado and her Project Delivery Team through Project Ridgeway, a fictional Army MILCON maintenance facility, from a customer’s first request to completion and closeout. Each case file pairs a chapter’s Military Programs doctrine with a people skill, through a branching scenario and a 6-question Knowledge Check.

Preview buildAll sixteen case files are complete and playable, one per PROSPECT 088 chapter. Content follows the PROSPECT 088 consolidated manuscript v0.10 (peer review draft) and is not yet validated for production. Dollar figures in Case Files 03, 07 and 10 are illustrative inputs taken from different chapters and are not one approved Ridgeway budget.
For Facilitators and Self-Paced Learners

Each case file runs about 25 to 30 minutes and stands on its own: training, reflection, a branching scenario of three or four decisions, and a formative Knowledge Check (4 of 6 to move on). The full course is roughly 8 hours and follows the order of the PROSPECT 088 chapters.

Recommended use: one or two case files per week as self-paced reinforcement, or individual case files as discussion starters in PDT, program or branch training. The storyline is continuous, and each case file works if used out of sequence.
